## The Setup: Bravo Team, the Ecliptic Express, and a Nightmare Train

S.T.A.R.S. Bravo Team is deployed into the Arklay Mountains to investigate a string of grisly murders. Their helicopter suffers engine failure, and the team scatters into the forest. Rebecca Chambers, the unit's young medic, finds a luxury train stopped on the tracks: the Ecliptic Express, an Umbrella-owned train whose passengers have been transformed into zombies.

Onboard, she discovers Edward Dewey, a fellow Bravo Team member, mortally wounded. He dies shortly after warning her about what is loose on the train. Rebecca also meets Billy Coen, a convicted murderer who escaped during a prisoner transfer when his escort was attacked. With the train suddenly lurching into motion, the two strangers are forced to work together.

## Rebecca and Billy: The Partnership That Drives the Plot

Billy Coen is a former Marine lieutenant sentenced to death for the murder of civilians in Africa. He insists he refused an unlawful order and was made a scapegoat. Rebecca, barely out of training, has every reason to turn him in — and spends most of the game deciding whether to believe him.

That tension is the emotional spine of the story. Rebecca is written as competent but inexperienced; Billy is the hardened soldier with nothing left to lose. Community reports often note that their dynamic carries the first half of the game, since the pacing slows considerably once the train crashes.

## The Umbrella Executive Training Center and Marcus's Revenge

After the train crash, Rebecca and Billy hike to the Umbrella Executive Training Center, a research and training facility hidden in the mountains. This is where the **Resident Evil 0 story explained** puzzle finally clicks into place.

Dr. James Marcus was one of Umbrella's founding scientists, alongside Ozwell E. Spencer. Marcus ran the training center and experimented with leeches, using them to develop the t-Virus from the Progenitor virus. Roughly a decade before the game's events, Spencer ordered Albert Wesker and William Birkin to assassinate Marcus and shut down his work.

What Spencer's team did not account for: Marcus's leeches absorbed his DNA and memories. The result was the Queen Leech, a creature that reconstructed itself in Marcus's image and set out to punish Umbrella. That is the "Marcus" who attacks Rebecca and Billy throughout the game — not a survivor, but a monster wearing a dead man's face.

## The Ending Explained: Queen Leech, Billy's Fate, and the Handoff to Resident Evil 1

In the underground laboratory, the Queen Leech sheds its human disguise and attacks. Rebecca and Billy destroy it, ending Marcus's revenge, but the facility is already tearing itself apart.

By this point, Rebecca has found the files proving Billy was set up. She chooses to let him go and reports him as killed in action, which is why Billy Coen never appears in a mainline Resident Evil game again. Player experience suggests this is the most debated part of the ending: Billy's survival is implied rather than shown.

The ending is deliberately a handoff. Rebecca arrives at the Spencer Mansion, where Resident Evil 1 begins, and the t-Virus that Marcus helped create is now loose in the Arklay Mountains. Wesker's role in the cover-up also sets up his reveal in the first game, while Birkin's ongoing research leads directly into Resident Evil 2.

Beating the game unlocks the Leech Hunter minigame, and the 2016 HD Remaster adds Wesker Mode, a what-if campaign that lets you play through the story as the series' most famous traitor. ## FAQ: Common Questions About the Prequel's Plot

**Is Resident Evil 0 a prequel?**
Yes. It takes place in July 1998, roughly a day before the mansion incident in Resident Evil 1. It is the earliest mainline entry in the timeline.

**Should I play Resident Evil 1 first?**
Either order works. Playing Resident Evil 1 first makes Rebecca's role land harder, but the Resident Evil 0 story explained in-game is self-contained enough for newcomers.

**Does Billy Coen come back?**
No. Billy does not appear in any later mainline game. His survival is strongly implied, but Rebecca's report lists him as dead, and Capcom has never revisited him.

**Who created the t-Virus?**
The game credits Dr. James Marcus, who derived it from the Progenitor virus using leech-based research at the Umbrella Executive Training Center — a detail that reframes everything Umbrella claimed about the outbreak.
