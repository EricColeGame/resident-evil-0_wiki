## Why the Laboratory Section Breaks So Many Runs

The laboratory is the final stretch of Resident Evil 0, and it's where most runs fall apart. You arrive with whatever ammo survived the Training Facility, a partner standing somewhere you didn't plan, and a boss lineup that punishes panic. This Resident Evil 0 laboratory walkthrough fixes that: a stage-by-stage route through Umbrella's research complex, the enemies you'll meet, and the order of operations that keeps both characters alive. Play it right and the lab becomes the most satisfying hour in the game.

The section is brutal for structural reasons, not just raw difficulty. Resident Evil 0 has no item boxes, so every resource you carry is a resource you consciously decided to carry. Partner swapping means one careless decision can strand your healer on the far side of the map. And the two hardest fights in the game — a Tyrant prototype and the Queen Leech — sit back to back here, with a countdown escape bolted on at the end.

Three systems decide whether you walk out:

- **No item boxes.** Dropped items stay where you leave them, so the lab rewards planned caches instead of hoarding.
- **Partner zapping.** Instant character swapping is a weapon — and a liability if your partner is set to follow into a hallway full of enemies.
- **Fire, not bullets, kills leeches.** Leech-type enemies reform after being downed unless you burn them.

## Prep Work: What to Bring Into the Lab

You cannot buy your way out of a bad inventory in this area. Before you commit to the lab's deeper wings, sort both characters so that each one has a clear job: one carries the heavy weapons, the other carries healing and utility. Anything you're not sure about should be dropped in a save room, not carried "just in case."

| Priority | Item | Why it matters in the lab |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Grenade Launcher with your strongest rounds | The best single answer to the Tyrant prototype; burst damage wins in tight rooms |
| 2 | Shotgun | Stops zombies that block doorways and stairwells |
| 3 | Molotov Cocktails or another fire source | The only reliable way to permanently remove leech enemies |
| 4 | First Aid Spray plus mixed herbs | Instant healing without burning time sorting inventory mid-fight |
| 5 | Ink Ribbons (on difficulties that use them) | Saves are a resource; budget them before the boss encounters |
| 6 | Two or three empty slots | Key items and puzzle pieces appear without warning |

If you're missing the heavy weapons, don't restart. The lab is still beatable with handguns, a shotgun, and disciplined avoidance — it just takes longer and demands cleaner movement. Available weapons, modes, and bonus content vary by platform and release, so check [Capcom's official Resident Evil series hub](https://www.residentevil.com/) if you're unsure what your version includes.

**Character roles matter more here than anywhere else in the game.**

| Job | Rebecca | Billy |
|---|---|---|
| Herb mixing and medicine prep | Yes — she carries the mixing set | No |
| Absorbing hits on the front line | Fragile | Sturdy |
| Melee and knockback | Weak | Strong |
| Best default role in the lab | Medic and item runner | Point guard and boss damage |

The single most useful habit in this area: park your partner in a room you've already cleared, then scout ahead alone. Partner AI that wanders into an uncleared corridor will burn your healing items faster than any boss.

## Resident Evil 0 Laboratory Walkthrough: The Route, Stage by Stage

The lab is best understood as five stages rather than a maze. Each one has a single objective, and each one punishes you for fighting more than you need to.

| Stage | Objective | Main threat | Key move |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Arrival and first save room | Bank progress, sort inventory | Zombies in corridors | Build a cache of spare ammo and healing in the save room |
| 2. Secured doors and keycards | Open the lab's locked wings | Leech enemies in narrow halls | Burn them or walk past — never trade bullets |
| 3. Mid-lab item shuffle | Move key items between wings | Partner AI drifting into danger | Park your partner in a cleared room, then scout alone |
| 4. The Tyrant prototype | Survive the mandatory boss | Heavy melee damage in a small arena | Spend your grenade rounds here, not later |
| 5. Final boss and escape | Kill the Queen Leech, then get out | Countdown plus respawning enemies | Run on the way out; do not stop to fight |

### Stage 1–2: Arrival, Saving, and the First Locked Doors

Clear the entry corridors methodically, then make the first save room your base of operations. Dump anything situational — spare ammo types, duplicate healing, puzzle items you've already used — and keep only what the next two rooms require. When you hit the secured doors, expect leech enemies in hallways too narrow to dodge comfortably. Fire is the answer; if you don't have it, back out and take another route.

### Stage 3: The Item Shuffle

This is where the no-item-box design bites. Key items must physically travel between wings, and the game rarely gives you a straight line. Two rules keep this stage painless: move one item at a time, and leave your partner somewhere already cleared before every trip. Community reports consistently name this stretch — not the bosses — as the part that ruins otherwise clean runs.

### Stage 4: The Tyrant Prototype

The Tyrant prototype is a bulky, fast, hard-hitting B.O.W. that closes distance quickly in a confined space. It is not a war of attrition you can win with a handgun. Open with your heaviest burst, keep circling to maintain distance, and heal the instant you take a hit rather than waiting for a safe window that may never come.

### Stage 5: Queen Leech and the Escape

The Queen Leech is the game's final boss and the payoff for everything you saved. Fight patiently: punish its recovery animations, keep moving laterally, and hold back one healing item for the escape. Once the countdown starts, treat every enemy as scenery — running past a zombie costs nothing, and fighting it can cost you the ending.

## Laboratory Enemies and Boss Fights

Knowing what each enemy wants is half the battle. The other half is accepting that some encounters are meant to be avoided.

| Enemy | Behavior | Best answer |
|---|---|---|
| Lab zombies | Slow, grabby, occasionally feign death | Handgun headshots or simply walk past |
| Leech enemies (Mimicry Marcus) | Mimic human silhouettes, grab and drain, reform when downed | Fire — Molotovs or incendiary rounds — or avoid entirely |
| Lurkers (amphibious B.O.W.s) | Leap from water and lower ledges | Shotgun at close range while backing away |
| Tyrant prototype | Charges and hits hard in tight spaces | Grenade Launcher and magnum-class weapons; keep distance |
| Queen Leech | Large final boss with wide, sweeping attacks | Your strongest remaining ammo, spacing, and patience |

**Boss comparison and fight plans:**

| Boss | Recommended loadout | Fight plan |
|---|---|---|
| Tyrant prototype | Grenade Launcher, magnum-class revolver | Burst it early, circle constantly, heal immediately after every hit |
| Queen Leech | Whatever heavy ammo remains | Stay mobile, punish recovery frames, save one heal for the escape |

Player experience suggests the Tyrant prototype causes more deaths than the final boss, simply because it arrives when your supplies are thinnest. If you have to choose which fight gets the good ammo, give it to the Tyrant.

## Mistakes That Get Players Killed in the Lab

Most failures in this area trace back to a handful of habits. Fix these and the section gets dramatically easier:

- **Treating leech enemies like zombies.** Bullets knock them down; only fire keeps them down.
- **Splitting up without clearing the room first.** Your partner will find the one enemy you missed.
- **Hoarding heavy ammo for a boss that never comes.** Finishing the game with a full grenade launcher is not a victory.
- **Leaving the partner on follow mode in a hallway.** Park them. Always.
- **Skipping the item cache.** A save room stocked with spare healing is worth more than three extra inventory slots.
- **Fighting during the escape sequence.** The clock, not the enemies, is the actual threat.

## FAQ

**How long does the Resident Evil 0 laboratory walkthrough section take?**
First-time players typically spend 45 to 75 minutes in the lab, including the two boss fights. On repeat runs with a stocked inventory and a known route, most finish it in 15 to 25 minutes.

**Do I really need fire to kill the leech enemies?**
Yes. Leech-type enemies reassemble after being knocked down by conventional weapons. Fire sources remove them permanently, and avoiding them entirely is a valid alternative if your supplies are thin.

**Which character should carry the healing items in the lab?**
Rebecca is the natural choice because she carries the mixing set and can combine herbs into stronger medicine. Billy should carry the heavy weapons and act as the front line, since he can absorb far more punishment.

**Is the lab different in the HD remaster or on higher difficulties?**
The layout and encounters are the same, but resource pressure increases sharply on harder settings, where saves and ammunition are scarcer. Unlockable bonus content varies by version, so it's worth checking your specific release before planning a challenge run.

Get the prep right, respect the leech enemies, and save your heaviest ammunition for the Tyrant prototype — do that, and the last hour of Resident Evil 0 stops being a wall and starts being the best part of the game.
