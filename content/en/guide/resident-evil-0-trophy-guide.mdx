## Why the Resident Evil 0 Trophy List Is Harder Than It Looks

Resident Evil 0 is the black sheep of the classic survival horror era, and its trophy list punishes anyone who treats it like a carbon copy of the first game. This Resident Evil 0 trophy guide exists because the title quietly breaks the series' most comforting rule: there are no item boxes. Every item you find stays exactly where you drop it, and two playable characters share a brutally tight inventory.

That one design decision turns a familiar checklist into a logistics puzzle. Add Hard mode, no-save conditions, a Leech Hunter side mode, and a bonus Wesker campaign, and the platinum becomes a multi-run project rather than a weekend sprint. The good news? Once you understand the routing logic, most of the list falls into place quickly.

Before you plan anything, it helps to sort the trophies by what they actually demand of you.

| Trophy Category | Typical Requirement | Difficulty | Biggest Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Story progression | Reach each major area and finish the campaign | Low | None |
| Difficulty clears | Complete the game on Hard | High | Ammo starvation |
| Endurance runs | Finish without saving or in a single sitting | High | Losing hours of progress |
| Side content | Clear Leech Hunter and Wesker Mode | Medium–High | Resource management |
| Cleanup | Files, herb mixes, specific kill methods | Low–Medium | Missable actions |

The categories matter because they overlap. A single careless run can invalidate three trophies at once, which is exactly why the order below is worth following.

## Platinum Roadmap: The Order That Saves You Hours

A good Resident Evil 0 trophy guide does not tell you to do everything at once. Trying to stack a no-save condition, a time limit, and Hard mode on your very first playthrough is the fastest way to burn out. Instead, treat each run as a layer.

| Step | Mode / Activity | Main Goal | Why It Belongs Here |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Normal playthrough | Learn maps, unlock Leech Hunter and Wesker Mode | Zero pressure, full story context |
| 2 | Leech Hunter | Bank a perfect leech collection | Unlocks bonus firepower for later runs |
| 3 | Hard playthrough | Difficulty and completion trophies | Bonus weapon softens the spike |
| 4 | Timed run | Speed-run trophy | Route is already memorized |
| 5 | Wesker Mode | Mode-specific trophy | Wesker's abilities make it fast |
| 6 | Cleanup | Files, herb mixes, odd kills | Mop up whatever remains |

Step 2 is the one most players skip, and it is a mistake. Leech Hunter is a short, self-contained challenge set inside the training facility, and clearing it with a full collection rewards you with a significantly stronger arsenal for the main campaign. Doing that before Hard mode is the single biggest time-saver in this entire Resident Evil 0 trophy guide.

## Core Mechanics That Make or Break Your Trophy Run

Three systems decide whether your trophy runs succeed or stall out.

**Partner zapping.** You control Rebecca Chambers and Billy Coen, and you can swap between them on the fly when they are together. Rebecca handles herb mixing and medical items. Billy has the physical strength to shove heavy crates and obstacles out of the way, plus his lighter for environmental puzzles. Learn who solves what before you commit to a route.

**Item dropping.** With no item boxes, the floor is your storage. Drop supplies in rooms you will pass through repeatedly rather than carrying everything. Items stay put, so a well-stocked "hub" room is better than a full inventory.

**Inventory limits.** Each character carries only six slots. That is twelve slots total, minus weapons, ammo, keys, and puzzle items. Never leave a safe room with a full inventory if you know a key item is ahead.

Herb mixing is the other mechanic worth memorizing. Rebecca can combine herbs, and the combinations are not interchangeable.

| Mix | Effect | Best Used For |
|---|---|---|
| Green | Small heal | Minor chip damage |
| Green + Green | Moderate heal | After a serious hit |
| Green + Green + Green | Full heal | Emergency recovery |
| Green + Red | Full heal, efficient | Boss fights |
| Green + Blue | Heal plus poison cure | Spider and Lurker areas |
| Green + Green + Blue | Large heal plus poison cure | Poison stacked with heavy damage |

Red herbs never work alone. Blue herbs only matter once poison enters the picture. Wasting a Red on a scratch is one of the most common beginner mistakes, and it will cost you on Hard mode.

## Hard Mode, No-Save, and Speed-Run Trophies: Survival Strategy

Hard mode in Resident Evil 0 does not just raise enemy health. Ammo drops thin out, healing items become scarcer, and mistakes compound across an entire session. The endurance and timed trophies then stack on top of that pressure.

The counter to all of it is threat prioritization. You cannot kill everything, so decide which enemies are worth the bullets.

| Enemy | Where It Appears | Threat Level | Best Answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zombies | Train, facility | Medium | Handgun headshots, knife on downed foes, or just run past |
| Leech Zombies | Facility, labs | High | Fire-based weapons; never let them corner you |
| Plague Crawlers | Facility corridors | Medium | Shotgun up close, keep distance otherwise |
| Eliminators | Facility | Very High | Shotgun, avoid open rooms, never get surrounded |
| Proto Tyrant | Laboratory | High | Heavy weapons, circle and retreat |
| Queen Leech | Final area | Very High | Save your strongest ammo, heal between phases |

Eliminators are the run-enders. Community reports consistently name them as the enemy that ruins no-save attempts, because a single bad room can drain your entire healing stock. If you are attempting an endurance run, plan your route to minimize Eliminator encounters rather than fighting through them.

For no-save trophies, the practical advice is scheduling, not skill. Player experience suggests treating each attempt as a single long session, keeping notes on which rooms you cleared, and never experimenting with a new route while the trophy is on the line.

For the speed-run trophy, the route you learned in step one is your script. Skip optional pickups, ignore non-essential files, and only detour for items that directly solve a puzzle or feed your strongest weapon. Many players report finishing comfortably under three hours once the route is memorized — but that number means nothing until you have the map in your head.

## Leech Hunter, Wesker Mode, and Cleanup Trophies

A Resident Evil 0 trophy guide that ignores the bonus modes is only doing half the job. Both extras are short, and both are far easier once the main campaign has taught you the layout.

| Extra Mode | How It Unlocks | What It Tests | Trophy Tip |
|---|---|---|---|
| Leech Hunter | Finish the main story once | Routing and ammo discipline | Split charms between both characters; clear the riskiest rooms first |
| Wesker Mode | Finish the main story once | Combat efficiency | Use Wesker's dash and charged attack to skip fights entirely |
| Cleanup pass | Always available | Attention to detail | Track files and herb mixes area by area |

In Leech Hunter, you hunt leech charms scattered across the facility while managing limited ammunition. Do not treat it as a combat mode — treat it as a scavenger hunt with teeth. Grab what you can reach safely, retreat, and return with better positioning. A full collection is the goal because it feeds directly into your Hard mode run.

Wesker Mode is the opposite experience. Playing as Albert Wesker with Rebecca in tow, you get movement and attack options the base campaign never gives you. It is the easiest place to knock out remaining combat-related trophies, so save it for after Hard mode rather than before.

Cleanup is where most platinums stall. Files are easy to miss in rooms you rushed through, and herb-mixing trophies require deliberate combinations you would never use naturally. Walk each area with a checklist, and confirm your progress on the results screen before moving on.

## FAQ: Resident Evil 0 Trophy Guide Questions

**How many playthroughs does the platinum actually require?**
Plan for three to four. A relaxed first run, a Hard mode run, a timed run, and a partial cleanup pass cover almost everything. Stacking the timed run with Hard mode is possible but punishing for anyone who has not already memorized the route.

**Can I skip Hard mode entirely?**
Only if you are willing to give up the platinum. Difficulty trophies typically sit on the critical path, and skipping them means the completion never fires. Follow this Resident Evil 0 trophy guide in order and Hard mode becomes manageable rather than miserable.

**What is the single hardest trophy on the list?**
Community consensus points to the no-save or endurance-style trophy, because it demands an uninterrupted session with no room for a bad Eliminator encounter. The speed-run trophy is more about route knowledge than raw difficulty.

**Does Wesker Mode count toward story trophies?**
Treat bonus modes as separate content. Clear the standard campaign for story and difficulty trophies, then use Wesker Mode to sweep up anything combat-related that is still outstanding.

Work through the roadmap in order, respect the six-slot inventory, and resist the urge to fight every enemy you see. The Resident Evil 0 trophy list rewards planning far more than reflexes — and once the route clicks, the platinum is far closer than the train ride suggests.
